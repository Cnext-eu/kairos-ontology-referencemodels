  - IATA-1R-DM-Ontology.domains-patch.ttl (generated) supplies rdfs:domain
    triples absent from the upstream mirror. The mirror encodes property
    domains as a non-standard owl:comment "Domain :ClassName"@en
    annotation instead of rdfs:domain (532 of 564 properties carry it;
    zero rdfs:domain triples exist). Any rdfs:domain-based consumer,
    including the Kairos toolkit, otherwise sees zero properties on every
    ONE Record class. Regenerate the patch via
    scripts/generate_iata_domain_patch.py on every refresh of this
    mirror -- do not hand-edit it, and do not hand-edit the vendor .ttl
    files either. Wired in via a catalog-v001.xml redirection; see gh#93.
    Not yet reported upstream to IATA-Cargo/ONE-Record.
